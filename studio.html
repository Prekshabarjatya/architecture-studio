The studio · Indore, Madhya Pradesh

A small studio that designs from the inside out.

Architecture and interiors, drawn by hand first and built with the people who draw them.

Our story(01)

We start with how a room is used. The walls come after.

Space-in began in Indore with a simple habit: before drawing a wall, spend a morning and an evening on the site and ask the people who will live or work there how their day actually goes.

That habit shaped everything else. Every plan is sketched by hand before it is drawn on a screen, every material is chosen for how it will age, and the architects who draw a building stay on site until the keys are handed over.
