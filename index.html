The studio(01)

Designed from the inside out. Shaped around the space in between.

Space-in is an architecture and interiors studio in Indore. We start with how a room is used: where the light falls at 8 am, where people gather, where they want to be alone. The walls come after. Every project is drawn, detailed and built by one team, from the first site visit to the handover of keys.
